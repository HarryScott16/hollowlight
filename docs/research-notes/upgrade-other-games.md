# How other games show upgrade tiers and branching

## Kingdom Rush (Ironhide)

- **Structure (verified):** in KR1, Frontiers and Origins, a tower upgrades three times, then the player picks one of two level-4 specialisations, each with buyable abilities (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Rush). Vengeance dropped the branch.
- **Levels 1-3 (unverified):** small steady steps on the same plinth and footprint: sturdier materials and a bit taller. Examples:
  - Archer: a wooden tower, then a stone base, then a banner or roof.
  - Barracks: the soldiers it sends out get heavier armour.
  - Mortar: the barrel grows.
  - Mage: the tower gets taller and brighter.
- **The level 4 branch (unverified):** where the big change is spent. Each branch keeps the slot and base size but gets its own silhouette, palette and crew acting out its role:
  - Rangers Hideout (leafy, hooded rangers) versus Musketeer Garrison (a stone fort).
  - Big Bertha (one huge barrel: mass) versus Tesla (a coil spire with arcs: a vertical spike of light).
  - Arcane Wizard (slender spire) versus Sorcerer (darker, summons an elemental).
- **Origins' druid branch:** one branch intensifies the original form (Arch-Druid Henge, still a stone circle). The other turns the theme into a living creature (Weirdwood, a treant). Source: search snippets of the fandom pages.
- **Abilities bought within a specialisation** mostly appear as new effects or summons, not redrawn towers (unverified).
- **Simple 2D sprites keep crowded fields readable** (the-avocado.org franchise retrospective).

## Clash of Clans (Supercell)

- **Redraws only at milestone levels, with small steps in between.**
- **Archer Tower** (fan sources, unverified):
  - a stone base;
  - then all the wood replaced by dark iron;
  - then silver plating turned gold.

  The archers on top change look on a separate schedule from the tower.
- **Walls are a textbook material ladder** (fan sources, unverified): cut stone, iron, carved gold, elixir crystal, lava, then dark stone with gold. The arc runs from humble, to precious, to magical, to elemental or dark.
- **Town Hall:** a different colour scheme at each milestone. At TH12, the look changes again as its inner weapon levels up (clasher.us).
- **Inferno Tower modes:** one big hole for single target versus three small holes for multiple targets. A small change of shape encodes the role (pocketgamer.com Inferno Tower guide).

## Clash Royale card evolutions

- **The same troop with an added accessory**, a reserved accent colour (purple) and a new effect. "Evolved Skeletons wear a cool hat" (supercell.com release notes; support.clashroyale.com).

## Defense Grid: The Awakening

- **About ten tower types at most** ("more than 10 started to get confusing"), with three visual levels each.
- **Generic pylons made towers small and hard to tell apart,** so each tower got a tall, distinct silhouette worked out in silhouette studies. Tower art was revised more than anything else in the game (gamedeveloper.com, "The Art of Defense Grid").

## League of Legends skin tiers

Each tier stacks a further channel (wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_skin):

1. texture only;
2. a new model;
3. new effects, animation and sound;
4. new voice and a full persona overhaul;
5. a model that evolves through several forms.

Impressiveness comes from stacking channels, and the top tier changes while you watch.

## World of Warcraft tier sets

- **The same armour model in four colourways.** The top (Mythic) one adds particle effects such as lightning or void wisps (siliconera.com).
- **Players complain when variants look alike.** The steps must be clearly distinguishable.

## Pokémon evolution lines

- **Silhouette and shrink tests** (creativebloq.com).
- **Signature features carry through a line,** while the creature grows larger and more angular (unverified).
- **Big palette changes weaken the link** between stages (fan analysis).
- **Eevee's branches** keep the body plan and change the element motif, colour and accessories.

## Vampire Survivors weapon evolutions

- **The evolved weapon keeps its base motion,** with a recolour, more area and a gameplay twist. Bloody Tear is still recognisably the whip (vampire.survivors.wiki).

## Arknights Elite promotion

- **The big reward goes on the portrait** (a new outfit). The small battle sprite often doesn't change (arknights.wiki.gg).

## Plants vs. Zombies 2

- **Glow plus a flash means a temporary super state** (Plant Food). Careful: don't overuse glow for permanent tiers (plantsvszombies.wiki.gg).

## Principles the agent drew from this

1. **Spend change unevenly:** small at tiers 1-2, big at the branch and the top tier.
2. **One identity anchor that never changes.**
3. **Early tiers climb a shared material ladder.**
4. **Upgrade the crew or core on its own schedule from the body.**
5. **The branch split changes shape language, not just colour:** one big thing versus many small ones, mass versus spire, the intensified original versus a living version of the theme.
6. **A reserved accent colour or motif per path,** over the shared base palette.
7. **Test at game zoom, in silhouette.** If two tiers match as black shapes, the step is too small.
8. **Make the top tier impressive by stacking channels** (constant motion, emitted light, particles, a jump in size), not only by adding detail.
9. **Leave a channel unused for tier 5** (a second form, a ground aura, a crown piece).
10. **Show small sub-upgrades as attachments, not redraws.**
11. **Keep the number of variants manageable:** a shared tier ladder, with one motif per path.
12. **Keep temporary power-up visuals (pulsing glow) separate from permanent tier visuals** (structure, material, silhouette).
